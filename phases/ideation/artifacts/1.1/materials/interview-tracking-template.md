## Column Definitions

### Basic Information
- **#**: Interview number (1-15)
- **Date**: Date interview was conducted (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **Persona**: Which persona (VP Sales, Sales Enablement, AE, Founder)
- **Company**: Company name
- **Role**: Interviewee's job title
- **Demo Volume/Mo**: Approximate monthly demo volume for their team

### Section 1: Problem Discovery

- **Problem Confirmed**: Did they acknowledge the demo scheduling/no-show problem? (Yes/No)
  - Maps to KPI I1.1: Problem Confirmation Rate

- **Pain Score (1-10)**: How painful is this problem for them?
  - 1 = Not painful at all, 10 = Critical business issue
  - Maps to KPI I1.2: Pain Intensity Score

- **Problem Last Occurred**: When did they last experience this problem?
  - <30 days / 30-90 days / >90 days / Never
  - Maps to KPI I1.3: Problem Recency

- **Workarounds Used**: Do they have active workarounds to address this problem?
  - Yes/No + brief description of what they're doing
  - Examples: "Calendly + manual follow-ups", "Dedicated SDR for scheduling", "Reminder emails"
  - Maps to KPI I1.4: Workaround Existence

### Section 2: Solution Exploration

- **Solution Interest (1-5)**: Interest level in the AI demo agent solution
  - 1 = Not interested, 5 = Want to try immediately
  - Maps to KPI I1.5: Solution Interest Score

- **Would Switch**: Would they switch from their current solution to this?
  - Yes / No / Maybe (if yes or maybe, note conditions)
  - Maps to KPI I1.6: Competitive Displacement

- **Outcome Alignment**: Does solution address their desired outcomes?
  - Yes / No (do they see it solving their problem?)
  - Maps to KPI I1.7: Outcome Alignment

### Section 3: Willingness to Pay

- **Has Budget**: Do they have budget for this category?
  - Yes / No / Would need to find budget
  - Maps to KPI I1.8: Budget Existence

- **Current Annual Spend**: How much do they currently spend annually on demo-related costs?
  - Include: scheduling tools, people time, demo engineers, etc.
  - Format: $XX,XXX
  - Maps to KPI I1.9: Current Spend

- **Purchase Intent (1-5)**: Likelihood to purchase in 60 days if available
  - 1 = Would not purchase, 5 = Ready to buy immediately
  - Maps to KPI I1.10: Purchase Intent Score

### Qualitative Data

- **Key Quotes & Notes**: Capture important quotes and insights
  - Direct quotes (use quotation marks)
  - Surprising insights
  - Red flags or concerns
  - Follow-up action items

## Quick KPI Calculation (Mid-Way Check)

After 5-7 interviews, do a quick check on key metrics:

| KPI | Formula | Your Score | Target | On Track? |
|-----|---------|------------|--------|-----------|
| I1.1: Problem Confirmation | (# who confirmed problem) / (# interviews) | __% | ≥70% | Yes / No |
| I1.2: Pain Intensity | (Sum of pain scores) / (# interviews) | __/10 | ≥7/10 | Yes / No |
| I1.3: Problem Recency | (# <30 days) / (# interviews) | __% | ≥60% | Yes / No |
| I1.5: Solution Interest | (Sum of interest scores) / (# interviews) | __/5 | ≥3.5/5 | Yes / No |
| I1.10: Purchase Intent | (Sum of intent scores) / (# interviews) | __/5 | ≥3.5/5 | Yes / No |

**If Not On Track**:
- Are you talking to the right personas?
- Are you following The Mom Test principles?
- Is the problem real but solution off?
- Should you adjust your ICP or pivot?
