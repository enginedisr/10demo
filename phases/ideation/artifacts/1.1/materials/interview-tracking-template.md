## Instructions

**How to use this template:**

1. **During each interview:** Take live notes in the detailed section below
2. **Immediately after:** Fill out the summary tracking table
3. **After all 10-15 interviews:** Submit this completed file to the analyzer agent
4. **Analyzer output:** Will calculate KPIs I1.1-I1.10 and provide pass/fail verdict

**Recording tips:**
- Capture direct quotes (useful for landing page copy later)
- Note body language and enthusiasm level
- Record exact pricing numbers from Van Westendorp questions
- Flag potential early customers (for follow-up)

## Column Definitions

| Column | Definition | Maps to KPI |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| **Pain (0-10)** | Interviewee's self-reported pain score | I1.1 Pain Score |
| **Workaround?** | Do they have active workarounds? (Y/N) | I1.2 Workaround Rate |
| **Last 30d?** | Experienced problem in last 30 days? (Y/N) | I1.3 Recency |
| **Solution Interest** | Strong/Medium/Weak reaction to solution | I1.4 Solution Resonance |
| **Would Switch?** | Would consider switching from current approach? (Y/N) | I1.5 Competitive Displacement |
| **Outcome Align?** | Solution outcomes align with their goals? (Y/N) | I1.6 Outcome Alignment |
| **Has Budget?** | Allocated budget exists for this category? (Y/N) | I1.7 Budget Existence |
| **Current Spend/mo** | Monthly spend on alternatives/workarounds ($) | I1.8 Current Spend |
| **Van Westendorp Prices** | 4 pricing thresholds from Q21-Q24 ($) | I1.9 Willingness to Pay |
| **Intent (1-5)** | Purchase intent score (Q25) | I1.10 Purchase Intent |
