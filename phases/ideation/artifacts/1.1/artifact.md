# Recipe 1.1 Interview Materials - 10Demo
## Problem Validation Interview Execution Package

---

## Overview

This package contains everything needed to conduct effective customer discovery interviews for 10Demo's problem validation phase (Recipe 1.1).

**Venture**: 10Demo - AI agent for instant, personalized product demos
**Target**: B2B SaaS companies with demo scheduling and no-show problems
**Interview Goal**: Validate problem severity, solution resonance, and willingness to pay

## Package Contents

### 1. Interview Execution Guide
**File**: `interview-execution-guide.md`

Comprehensive guide including:
- **4 Target Personas** with detailed profiles and recruitment recommendations
  - VP of Sales (Primary Buyer) - 5-6 interviews
  - Sales Enablement Leader (Influencer) - 3-4 interviews
  - Account Executive (End User) - 2-3 interviews
  - Founder/CEO (Early-Stage) - 1-2 interviews

  - Section 1: Problem Discovery (Q1-Q8, 20 min)
  - Section 2: Solution Exploration (Q9-Q16, 20 min)
  - Section 3: Willingness to Pay (Q17-Q27, 20 min)

- **The Mom Test Principles** embedded throughout
  - Talk about their life, not your idea
  - Ask about specifics in the past, not hypotheticals
  - Listen 70%, talk 30%
  - Don't pitch - present neutrally

## How to Use This Package

### Week 1-2: Recruitment & Scheduling

1. **Review the 4 personas** in the execution guide
2. **Start outreach using templates** provided
   - Target: 20-30 outreach messages
   - Goal: 10-15 scheduled interviews
3. **Use recruitment pipeline tracker** to manage status
4. **Schedule interviews** using Calendly/similar tool

### Week 3-4: Conduct Interviews

1. **Before each interview**:
   - Review interview script
   - Research interviewee on LinkedIn
   - Open tracking template

2. **During interview** (30 min total):
   - Follow 27-question script
   - Take notes in real-time
   - Record with permission

3. **After each interview** (15 min):
   - Complete tracking template immediately
   - Send thank you + gift card
   - Add referrals to pipeline

4. **Mid-way check** (after 5-7 interviews):
   - Calculate quick KPIs
   - Review patterns in insights section
   - Adjust persona mix if needed

### Week 5: Analysis & Next Steps

1. **Complete all 10-15 interviews**
2. **Review tracking template for completeness**
3. **Submit to Recipe 1.1 Analyzer Agent**:
   - Completed tracking template
   - Additional notes/recordings
   - Your observations

4. **Analyzer outputs**:
   - All 10 KPIs calculated (I1.1 - I1.10)
   - Problem Validation status (Pass/Fail)
   - Persona insights and recommendations
   - Next steps based on results

## The Mom Test Quick Reference

Keep these principles in mind during every interview:

✅ **DO**:
- Ask about specific past experiences
- Listen 70%, talk 30%
- Dig into emotions and consequences
- Get specific numbers and timelines
- Ask "why" to find root causes
- Seek commitments, not compliments

❌ **DON'T**:
- Pitch your solution
- Ask hypothetical questions
- Accept generic answers
- Lead the witness
- Ignore red flags
- Talk more than you listen

## Interview Logistics

**Recommended Setup**:
- **Tool**: Zoom or Google Meet (use video)
- **Duration**: 30 minutes (25 min interview + 5 min buffer)
- **Recording**: Always ask permission
- **Note-taking**: Use tracking template live or immediately after
- **Scheduling**: Use Calendly with morning/late afternoon slots
- **Follow-up**: Thank you email within 24 hours

**Best Practices**:
- Avoid Mondays (busy) and Fridays (low show rate)
- Send reminder 24 hours before interview
- Have their LinkedIn and company website open
- Test tech 15 minutes before call
- Keep camera on to build rapport

## Question Categories

### Problem Discovery (Q1-Q8)
Purpose: Validate problem exists, is severe, and is recent

**Key Questions**:
- Q2: "Tell me about the last time a prospect requested a demo"
- Q4: "On a scale of 1-10, how painful is this problem?"
- Q5: "What have you tried to solve this?"

**What You're Listening For**:
- Specific stories, not hypotheticals
- Pain score ≥7/10
- Active workarounds (indicates real pain)
- Problem occurred <30 days ago

### Solution Exploration (Q9-Q16)
Purpose: Assess solution resonance and competitive fit

**Key Questions**:
- Q9: "What's your immediate reaction to this concept?"
- Q11: "How does this compare to what you're doing today?"
- Q13: "What concerns would you have about using AI?"

**What You're Listening For**:
- Genuine excitement vs. politeness
- Clear advantage over current solution
- Willingness to switch
- Objections you can overcome

### Willingness to Pay (Q17-Q27)
Purpose: Validate budget and establish pricing range

**Key Questions**:
- Q18: "What do you currently spend on demo-related tools and people?"
- Q21-Q24: Van Westendorp pricing questions
- Q26: "If available in 60 days, how likely would you be to purchase?"

**What You're Listening For**:
- Budget exists and is accessible
- Current spend ≥$50K/year
- Pricing reactions inform your pricing strategy
- Purchase intent ≥3.5/5

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### 1. Pitching Instead of Learning
❌ "We built this amazing AI that will revolutionize demos"
✅ "Tell me about your demo process today"

### 2. Accepting Generic Answers
❌ Them: "Yeah, demos are challenging"
✅ You: "Tell me about the last time you had a demo no-show"

### 3. Avoiding Money Conversations
❌ Skipping pricing questions because it's uncomfortable
✅ "What do you currently spend on solving this problem?"

### 4. Talking More Than Listening
❌ Explaining features for 15 minutes
✅ Ask question, shut up, let them talk

### 5. Ignoring Red Flags
❌ Them: "That's interesting" (flat tone) → You: "Great, so you'd buy it?"
✅ Probe deeper: "What specifically interests you about it?"

### 6. Interviewing Only Friendly Personas
❌ Only talking to people who already love your idea
✅ Interview skeptics and people who use competitors

### 7. Leading the Witness
❌ "Don't you think demo no-shows are a huge problem?"
✅ "How often do you experience demo no-shows?"

## Questions or Issues?

If during execution you encounter:

- **Low response rates**: Try warm intros, communities, or adjust messaging
- **Scheduled no-shows**: Send reminders, offer flexible rescheduling
- **Generic answers**: Follow The Mom Test more strictly, probe deeper
- **Defensive interviewees**: Build more rapport, emphasize learning not selling
- **Conflicting feedback**: Document patterns, let analyzer identify segments

**Remember**: The goal is to learn the truth, not to validate your assumptions. If the problem isn't real or the solution doesn't resonate, that's valuable information that saves you from building the wrong thing.
