| KPI | Target | What It Measures |

## Target Personas Summary

### Persona 1: VP Sales / Head of Sales (40% of interviews)
- **Segment:** Mid-Market to Enterprise (50-500 employees)
- **Why:** Primary economic buyer, owns demo metrics and budget
- **Focus:** Business impact, ROI, pricing, purchase decision

### Persona 2: SDR / Account Executive (25% of interviews)
- **Segment:** SMB to Mid-Market (20-200 employees)
- **Why:** End user, experiences daily pain, influences adoption
- **Focus:** Workflow friction, time savings, solution fit

### Persona 3: RevOps / SalesOps Manager (25% of interviews)
- **Segment:** Mid-Market to Enterprise (100-1000 employees)
- **Why:** Technical influencer, owns integrations and process
- **Focus:** CRM integration, data quality, total cost of ownership

### Persona 4: SaaS Founder / CEO (10-15% of interviews)
- **Segment:** SMB (5-50 employees)
- **Why:** Economic buyer, personally delivers demos, high urgency
- **Focus:** Founder time savings, budget constraints, self-service

## Interview Structure

### Section 1: Problem Discovery (20 min, Q1-Q8)
- Validate problem existence and severity
- Probe for workarounds (proof of urgency)
- Quantify business impact and cost

### Section 2: Solution Exploration (20 min, Q9-Q16)
- Present solution concept neutrally (don't pitch)
- Assess solution resonance and competitive positioning
- Check outcome alignment

### Section 3: Willingness to Pay (20 min, Q17-Q27)
- Establish budget existence and current spend
- Van Westendorp pricing questions (4-price methodology)
- Assess purchase intent and evaluation process

## The Mom Test Core Principles

**This interview methodology follows The Mom Test principles:**

1. ✅ Talk about their life, not your idea
2. ✅ Ask about specifics in the past, not opinions about the future
3. ✅ Listen 70% of the time, talk 30%
4. ✅ Present solution neutrally—don't pitch
5. ✅ Look for evidence (time/money spent), not enthusiasm
6. ✅ Dig into workarounds (proves problem urgency)
7. ✅ Discount compliments, trust revealed preferences

## Important Notes

### This Package is Preparation, Not Analysis
- **Your role as founder:** Conduct 10-15 interviews personally
- **Analyzer agent role:** Calculate KPIs and provide verdict
- **DO NOT skip interviews:** The goal is customer discovery, not validation

### The Mom Test Mindset
- You are NOT trying to validate your idea (confirmation bias trap)
- You ARE trying to learn if the problem is real, urgent, and expensive
- Trust evidence (time/money spent) over enthusiasm ("great idea!")
- Be prepared to hear that the problem doesn't matter (valuable learning)

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Pitching your solution (you're learning, not selling)
- ❌ Asking hypothetical questions ("would you use this?")
- ❌ Talking more than 30% of the time
- ❌ Skipping hard questions about budget and purchase intent
- ❌ Accepting compliments as validation ("that's interesting!")

## Questions or Issues?

If you encounter any issues during interview execution:

1. **Recruitment challenges:** Try multiple channels, adjust incentives, leverage warm intros
2. **Low response rates:** Improve personalization, emphasize learning (not selling), increase incentive
3. **Wrong personas responding:** Tighten targeting filters, be explicit about ideal interviewee
4. **Interview running long:** Practice pacing, use timer, skip less critical probing questions
5. **Interviewees being too polite:** Apply The Mom Test more rigorously, ask about past behavior
